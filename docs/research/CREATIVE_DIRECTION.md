# Creative Direction

The question from the brief: Yash's world is built around an origin point. What is Akshita's built around?

The research answered it before I did. Read her 21 posts in a row and one habit stands out: **every post ends in a list of names.** Mentors, co-interns, seniors, teammates, the organisers who handed her the mic. She doesn't describe connection; she does it, in writing, every time. Her four strongest "top skills" are people skills. Her arc runs from the quiet student in the back of class (her words) to the anchor on stage.

Three directions follow, each radically different.

---

## A. THE LOOM — "Woven"  ✅ selected

**Metaphor.** Her career is cloth. The **warp** (threads held taut on the loom) are the people, communities and skills around her. The **weft** is Akshita herself: one gold thread that travels across them, row after row. Every moment of her story is a row where her thread crosses others. A knot is a place where something was made together.

**Why her.** It is literally how she writes: each moment of her LinkedIn is a crossing of named threads. And Indore sits a short drive from Maheshwar, one of India's great handloom towns. The metaphor is local, not imported.

**Landing.** Darkness. A single gold point (her, the shuttle) draws one thread across the screen; it remains alone for a beat (the quiet seat). Then vertical threads of light drop in one by one, labelled with her actual communities, and her thread starts to weave through them. Her name resolves out of the weave. The threads are plucked by the pointer: touch one and it vibrates and glows, naming what it is.

**Navigation.** One gold thread runs down the page edge, carrying the visitor through seven sections named in weaving terms (First thread → Warp & weft → The knot → Swatches → Selvedge → Care label → Cast on). Click any marker to travel.

**LinkedIn as story engine.** "Warp & weft" is the loom: rows are her 14 key LinkedIn moments in date order, columns are threads (people groups, skills, places). Tap a thread → it lights through every moment it touched. Tap a row → the moment opens: what happened, her role, who she credited, a line in her words, and a link to the original post.

**Projects.** "Swatches": each project is a woven fabric sample generated from its own data (stack = warp colours, team size = weft density, duration = length).

**Motion language.** Tension and release. Threads are strings with spring physics; sparks of light run along threads when connections form; nothing pops in, everything is pulled.

**Visual system.** Night loom: deep plum-black ground, a single saffron-gold for Akshita, three dyed thread colours for people/skills/places (madder rose, indigo, teal). High-contrast serif display (Gloock), clean grotesk body, small mono labels like weaving drafts.

**Mobile.** The loom rotates: moments become a vertical list, threads become a horizontal chip rail. Plucking works by touch-drag. Canvas density drops automatically.

**Difficulty.** High — custom canvas physics + data-driven SVG loom. **Risks:** could feel crafty/soft rather than confident; mitigated by sharp type, darkness, gold light and a fast, certain motion style.

## B. ON AIR — "The Mic Is Live"

**Metaphor.** A broadcast studio. She's the voice: the site opens on a dark stage, a mic, the "On Air" light flicks on, and her headline is spoken as kinetic captions. Experiences are "segments" on a running order; posts are transcripts; the waveform of her voice is the navigation scrubber.

**Why her.** "Public Speaker" in her headline; anchor of the Symbiosis Skill Hackathon; representing SUAS at SLC.

**Landing.** Studio dark, rec light, a waveform builds from the pointer's movement; captions type her headline.

**LinkedIn.** Each post becomes a segment card in a rundown; "Read the transcript" opens it.

**Weakness.** Speaking is one part of her, and the metaphor pulls everything toward performance. Teaching, building and teamwork fit awkwardly. Waveforms/terminals also drift close to Yashord's console-and-sound idiom.

## C. FIELD OF LIGHT — "Just Start"

**Metaphor.** A dark field that lights one lamp per yes. Built on her "4 internships in 15 days" post: the visitor walks a path in near-darkness; each application, rejection, offer and stage is a lamp that lights as you pass, until the whole field glows. WebGL 3D path with depth fog.

**Why her.** Her most personal story is about courage and momentum; the closing "Just start." is a natural ending.

**LinkedIn.** Lamps are posts; the field is chronology.

**Weakness.** One strong narrative, but it's linear and about her alone, so it underplays the people she credits in every post. Heavy 3D for weak phones.

## Decision

**A. The Loom.** It is the only direction where the metaphor and the evidence are the same thing: her LinkedIn is already a record of threads crossing. It also absorbs B and C as rows inside it. Her stage moments are knots on the "voice" thread, and "Just start." becomes the first thread of the opening and the last words of the ending, so the site closes where it began.

**Identity test.** Replace her name and the loom collapses: the threads are labelled with her mentors, her teams, her communities, her dates.
